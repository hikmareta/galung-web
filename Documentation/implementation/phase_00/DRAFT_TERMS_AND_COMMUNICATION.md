## A. Terms of Service — proposed customer copy

1. **Provider and contact.** Galung.id is operated by `[VERIFIED LEGAL ENTITY]`, `[REGISTERED ADDRESS]`. Contact: `[SUPPORT EMAIL]`; business hours Monday–Friday, 09:00–17:00 WIB, excluding `[APPROVED HOLIDAY CALENDAR]`.
2. **Service.** Galung Basic and Pro provide the features, device allowance and term displayed in the approved plan description at checkout. The order records the selected policy version. Additional Song Library and institutional products are excluded unless expressly purchased under a future agreement.
3. **Price and term.** The exact IDR amount, plan, term, and any legally applicable tax appear on the invoice before payment. A missing price, company identity, approved bank account or unpublished plan cannot be ordered; the page says “Harga belum tersedia”. Initial and late-renewal access starts when payment has been independently approved and a usable signed license has been issued and made available. An early renewal extends the existing paid term. A monthly term is 30 calendar days and an annual term is 365 calendar days, subject to contract reconciliation and approved checkout wording.
4. **Manual transfer.** Payment is made only to the company beneficiary shown on the issued invoice. Bank or intermediary fees and conversion may reduce the amount received; the customer should use the invoice reference. A transfer notice or uploaded proof is not confirmation that funds arrived and does not activate access. The finance team verifies actual bank credit before approving; a separate authorized step issues the license. If the credited amount differs, support contacts the customer under the published shortfall policy.
5. **Late transfer.** If funds arrive after an invoice expires, contact `[HELP CENTER]` with the order reference. Funds are manually reviewed; access is not activated merely because a transfer was initiated or proof uploaded.
6. **License and offline use.** Device and concurrent-use limits are those shown for the purchased policy version. Temporary offline use is limited to 14 days after the last successful online validation and cannot exceed authorized subscription validity, including an eligible 72-hour grace period. Abuse, fraud or a security incident may require suspension under a separately approved process.
7. **Refunds and reversals.** Eligibility, partial-period treatment, destination account verification, processing time and effects on an issued license remain `[REFUND POLICY PENDING FINANCE/LEGAL APPROVAL]`. Consumer rights under applicable law are not waived by this draft.
8. **Changes and disputes.** Material plan or terms changes apply prospectively with notice; historical invoices and purchased policy snapshots remain preserved. Questions and disputes may be sent to `[SUPPORT EMAIL]`. The governing-law and dispute forum clause requires `[LEGAL REVIEW]`.

## B. Privacy Notice — proposed customer copy

1. **Controller.** `[VERIFIED LEGAL ENTITY AND CONTACT]` controls the customer data needed for account, billing, manual payment review, licensing, security and support.
2. **Categories.** Account/contact details; invoice identity; transfer references and private proof files; payment-review/audit records; pseudonymous device keys and validation events; support correspondence. Marketing consent is separate and optional.
3. **Purposes and access.** Data is used to provide the contracted service, verify actual funds, issue licenses, prevent abuse, meet legal obligations and answer requests. Finance staff access bank evidence; licensing staff access entitlement data; access is role limited and audited. Raw bank documents are not public.
4. **Retention.** The owner proposes 12 months for proof, financial, audit and device data. The final schedule must state the start event per category and account for applicable legal preservation duties, disputes and deletion rights; this paragraph is not an automatic deletion promise until Legal approves the schedule.
5. **Rights and requests.** Customers may contact `[PRIVACY CONTACT]` for access, correction, export or deletion requests. Some records may need lawful preservation; decisions and reasons should be communicated to the customer.
6. **International access and processors.** `[HOSTING LOCATION, STORAGE/EMAIL PROCESSORS, INTERNATIONAL TRANSFER SAFEGARDS]` require review and disclosure before publication.

## Legal review notes

- Indonesian Consumer Protection Law limits unfair standard clauses and recognizes remedies; refund terms must be reviewed before publication. [UU 8/1999](https://peraturan.bpk.go.id/Details/45288/uu-no-8-tahun-1999).
- Personal Data Protection Law ties deletion/destruction to retention purpose and legal conditions; the proposed 12 months needs category-specific legal validation. [UU 27/2022](https://peraturan.bpk.go.id/Details/229798/uu-no-27-tahun-2022%29).
- Electronic service and transaction terms require review under [PP 71/2019](https://peraturan.bpk.go.id/Details/122030/Pp-No-71-Tahun-2019).
